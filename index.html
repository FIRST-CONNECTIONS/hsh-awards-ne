About Us

A First Connections Initiative

The NE High Street Heroes Awards is brought to you by First Connections — a trading group spearheaded by two passionate North East business leaders, connecting, celebrating and amplifying business across the region.

From free networking events and community initiatives to shining a spotlight on the heroes of our high streets — First Connections is on a mission to make the North East extraordinary.

2026 Awards

Award Categories

Ten prestigious categories celebrating the very best of North East high street life. Nominations are free and open to all.

Category 01
🛍️

Retailer of the Year

Recognising independent and chain retailers who deliver exceptional products, service, and shopping experiences on our high streets.

Key Criteria
  • Outstanding customer service
  • Community engagement
  • Innovation in retail
Category 02
🍽️

Hospitality Hero

Celebrating cafés, restaurants, and pubs that go above and beyond to create memorable experiences for visitors and locals alike.

Key Criteria
  • Quality of food, drink & service
  • Atmosphere and ambiance
  • Local sourcing and community spirit
Category 03
🏅

Community Champion

Honouring individuals or organisations making an extraordinary difference to their local community and high street.

Key Criteria
  • Positive community impact
  • Voluntary contributions
  • Inspiring leadership
Category 04
💻

Digital Innovator

Rewarding businesses using digital tools and technology to enhance their high street presence and customer experience.

Key Criteria
  • Creative use of technology
  • Online-offline integration
  • Customer engagement innovation
Category 05
🌿

Sustainability Leader

Spotlighting businesses and individuals leading the way in sustainable practices on our high streets.

Key Criteria
  • Environmental initiatives
  • Waste reduction efforts
  • Sustainable sourcing
Category 06
⭐

Customer Experience Excellence

Celebrating businesses that consistently deliver outstanding customer experiences that keep people coming back.

Key Criteria
  • Exceptional service standards
  • Customer feedback and reviews
  • Accessibility and inclusivity
Category 07
🚀

Rising Star

Recognising new businesses or emerging talent that have made an impressive impact on their local high street.

Key Criteria
  • Business growth trajectory
  • Community contribution
  • Innovation and fresh ideas
Category 08
👔

Employer of the Year

Honouring businesses that invest in their people through training, wellbeing, and creating a great place to work.

Key Criteria
  • Staff development programmes
  • Employee wellbeing initiatives
  • Inclusive workplace culture
Category 09
🏪

Independent Business of the Year

Celebrating the independent businesses that are the backbone of our high streets — the ones who bring character, choice, and heart to our towns.

Key Criteria
  • Unique offering and identity
  • Customer loyalty and reputation
  • Contribution to high street vibrancy
Category 10
🏆

High Street of the Year

Recognising an entire high street that has shown outstanding community spirit, resilience, and collective effort to thrive.

Key Criteria
  • Collaborative community initiatives
  • Diversity of businesses
  • Overall vibrancy and appeal

How Voting Works

Our process is inclusive, transparent and community-driven — every vote counts.

1

Think of Your Hero

Think of a local business or individual who deserves recognition for what they bring to the North East.

2

Pick a Category

Choose the most fitting award category from our 10 categories — you can nominate across multiple.

3

Submit Your Vote

4

Expert Shortlisting

5
