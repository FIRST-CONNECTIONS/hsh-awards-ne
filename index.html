Our Story & Purpose

About the Awards

A regional recognition campaign celebrating the businesses, people, and organisations making a meaningful difference to North East high streets and town centres.

What We're Here For

Purpose-Led
Recognition

The North East High Street Heroes Awards is designed to shine a light on those creating vibrant, welcoming, and resilient local places — while encouraging stronger collaboration between businesses, communities, councils, BIDs, and regional partners.

These aren't just awards. They're a platform for positive change, community pride, and a stronger regional story.

Core Purpose
  • Recognise local business excellence and community impact
  • Celebrate the people helping high streets thrive
  • Generate positive regional publicity and public engagement
  • Strengthen relationships with local businesses and stakeholders
  • Create a platform for sponsorship, partnership, and collaborative campaigns
  • Help reshape public perception of the high street as a place to play, work, visit, and invest in
Changing the Narrative

A Different Kind of
High Street Story

Too often, mainstream media coverage focuses on decline, closures, and negative headlines. That creates an incomplete — and often misleading — picture of what is really happening at a local level.

"The future of the high street is not defined by multinational brands alone, but by the independent businesses, local employers, hospitality venues, community champions, and place-makers who keep town centres active and relevant."

The High Street Heroes Awards is designed to challenge that narrative — spotlighting the energy, innovation, resilience, and community value that exists across local high streets every day.

Built for the Whole Community

The awards are open to everyone contributing to North East high street life — from sole traders to community groups.

🤝

Councils, BIDs, and regional organisations investing in place and community.

Benefit 02

Regional Visibility

Increase brand visibility through a trusted local campaign with PR, media, and engagement reach.

Benefit 03

Practical Support

Support local businesses in a visible and practical way that builds real goodwill with communities.

Benefit 04

Stakeholder Goodwill

Build stronger relationships with communities, councils, BIDs, and regional stakeholders.

Benefit 05

Scalable Platform

Benefit 06

Positive Storytelling

Strategic Value

More Than an
Awards Night

Within the wider North East growth strategy, the awards act as a community engagement and partnership vehicle. They help create local momentum, open doors to new relationships, and provide a strong platform for media coverage, business outreach, and regional storytelling.

In short, the North East High Street Heroes Awards is a celebration of local impact — and a practical campaign for driving awareness, engagement, and partnership across the region, while helping redefine how high streets are seen by the public, partners, and the media.
