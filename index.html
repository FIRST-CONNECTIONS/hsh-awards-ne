About Us

A First Connections Initiative

The NE High Street Heroes Awards is brought to you by First Connections — a trading group spearheaded by two passionate North East business leaders, connecting, celebrating and amplifying business across the region.

From free networking events and community initiatives to shining a spotlight on the heroes of our high streets — First Connections is on a mission to make the North East extraordinary.

Andrew Bartlett
Co-Founder
Sam Binns
Co-Founder

Empowering North East Communities Through Recognition

2026 Awards

Award Categories

Ten prestigious categories celebrating the very best of North East high street life. Nominations are free and open to all.

Category 01
🛍️

Best Independent Retailer

Celebrating shops that bring character, personality and community to our high streets — the businesses that make every town unique.

Category 02
🍽️

Best Food & Drink Experience

Restaurants, cafes, pubs and bars elevating the local dining and drinking scene across the North East.

Category 03
💅

Best Health & Beauty

Salons, spas, barbershops and wellness businesses making a real difference to people's lives every day.

Category 04
🌟

Community Champion

An individual going above and beyond for their local community — the unsung hero who makes everyone's life better.

Category 05
🚀

Best New Business

New ventures that have made an immediate, impressive and lasting impact on their local high street.

Category 06
♻️

Best Sustainable Business

Businesses leading the way in environmental responsibility and sustainable practices across the region.

Category 07
🎨

Best Creative & Arts

Studios, galleries, makers and creative businesses enriching the cultural life of North East communities.

Category 08
🏋️

Best Health & Fitness

Gyms, sports clubs, studios and health services championing active and healthy communities across the NE.

Category 09
💼

Best Service Business

Professional service providers — from accountants to solicitors — who make the high street work for everyone.

Category 10
🥇

High Street Hero of the Year

The ultimate accolade — awarded to the very best of the North East. The one who embodies everything a High Street Hero should be.

How Voting Works

Our process is inclusive, transparent and community-driven — every vote counts.

1
